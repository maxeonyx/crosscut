# Discover: "What are we not thinking about?"

This mode is horizon expansion. It looks for concerns that are not on the
map yet. It persists nothing unless the human asks.

1. **Look at the real projects**, as in [setup.md](setup.md) step 1.
2. **Read `crosscut/concerns/`, if it exists.** What the map does not have a
   row for matters more than what it has. Ask what the existing concerns
   take for granted.
3. **Walk [../catalogue.md](../catalogue.md) and
   [../reservoirs.md](../reservoirs.md).** For each seed that is not on the
   map, ask whether its user stories touch these projects, and what it would
   look like here.
4. **Search the ecosystem for asymmetries:**
   - What does one project have, or do, that its siblings don't?
   - What do they all do differently for no reason?
   - What does every one of them lack?
5. **Try the crossings:** a kind of project × a moment in its life × a
   stakeholder, for example "the home server × losing the machine × a family
   member who can't fix it".

For each discovery, give:
- the concern's name and its user story;
- what it would look like here;
- a rough map row for each project;
- why it may matter now, and how confident you are.

Rank by leverage, not by severity. A concern whose gap could be filled across
five projects with one shared change comes first.

This is not a bug hunt. If the answer to "what are we not thinking about?" is
a list of defects, you have answered a different question.

End by offering to establish any of these as a persistent concern. Do not do
it unasked.
