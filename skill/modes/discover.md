# Discover: "What are we not thinking about?"

This mode is pure horizon expansion. It persists nothing unless the human
asks.

1. Look at the real thing, as in [setup.md](setup.md) step 1. Discovery
   without evidence produces generic advice, which is worthless here.
2. Read [../reservoirs.md](../reservoirs.md), then write your own reservoir
   for this place. It should be larger than feels necessary, and grounded in
   what you observed.
3. If `crosscut/concerns/` exists, read it. What is *not* represented
   matters more than what is. Ask what the existing concerns take for
   granted.
4. Try the crossings: shape × moment × concern. Also look beyond single
   repositories, at relationships, accounts, machines, time and cost.
5. Keep only what is specific and plausible. For each discovery, give:
   - what you saw, and where;
   - why it may matter *here*, and for whom;
   - how confident you are, and what would change your mind;
   - the lightest way to get a better view, or to make it go away.
6. Rank by leverage, not by severity. One structural change that dissolves
   three concerns beats three separate findings.

Lead with the one or two findings the human is least likely to have thought
of. It is fine, and often right, to say "this applies weakly here, so I would
not track it."

End by offering to make any of these a persistent concern. Do not do it
unasked.
